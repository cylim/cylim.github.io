Seals (/seals/, /favicon.svg, /favicon.ico, /apple-touch-icon.png)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Drawn for this site. The small-seal (小篆) letterforms follow public-domain reference
drawings on Wikimedia Commons, "Shuowen seal script characters (SVG)":

  林  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E6%9E%97-seal.svg  Alexbdf, public domain
  木  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E6%9C%A8-seal.svg  Wargaz, public domain

The 林 seal is traced by hand after the 林 and 木 drawings.

Adding an asset
---------------
Every third-party model, texture, sound or icon gets an entry here: what, where it is
used, author, source URL and licence (stack.md §4). Prefer CC0.


Sound
-----
None to credit. Every sound on the site (wind, pines, stream, cabin hum, door creak, guqin,
chime, seal) is synthesised in the browser at run time by src/audio; no recordings or
third-party samples ship.
